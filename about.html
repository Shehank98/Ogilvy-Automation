How We Work

Every automation goes through a structured 5-step process — from your first conversation to ongoing support.

1
🔍

Discovery

Starts with a consultation

We sit down with you (in person or via Teams) to understand the problem, its scope, and your goals. This session is free and typically takes 30 minutes.

2
📊

Analysis

Our process analyst maps the current workflow in detail — every step, decision, tool, and hand-off. We identify bottlenecks and automation opportunities.

3
✏️

Design

We design the automation architecture and get your sign-off before writing a single line of code. You'll see exactly what we're building and how it will work.

4
🔧

Build & Test

Our developers build the system and run it through thorough QA — including edge cases, error handling, and user-acceptance testing with a small group from your team.

5
🚀

Deploy & Support

We launch the system, train your team, and monitor it closely. Ongoing support and updates are handled via our bug-reporting portal — we're here for the long run.
