Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I get confirmed?
Within 24 business hours of submission. Our team reviews all requests during working hours (Monday–Friday). You'll receive an email confirmation with your Teams meeting link.
Can I change my preferred time after submitting?
Yes. You can check your request status on the status page and contact us from there, or reply to the confirmation email you receive.
What if my preferred slot isn't available?
If your preferred time isn't available, our admin will propose an alternative that works for both sides. That's why we ask for an alternative date and time in the form — it speeds up the process.
Is this really free?
Yes, completely free with no strings attached. The Ogilvy Automation Team is an internal service. Consultations are part of how we support colleagues across the business at no charge.
What happens during the meeting?
It's a relaxed 30-minute Teams call where we discuss your automation needs, identify opportunities, and explore solutions. You'll leave with a clear next step — whether that's a formal system request or a quick win you can implement immediately.
